# 🛡️ Centralized Hacking Techniques & Technical Guides

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is aimed at providing **technical guides and structured knowledge** on a wide range of **hacking and cybersecurity topics**.  
Its primary focus is on **advanced attack surfaces**, with special emphasis on:

- **Active Directory**
- **Web Services & Web Applications**

Additional topics will be added over time to expand coverage across multiple domains of offensive security.

The **ultimate goal** is to **centralize hacking techniques, methodologies, and concepts** into a single, well-organized knowledge base for learning, research, and professional growth.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Centralize fragmented hacking knowledge into one repository  
- Provide **clear, technical, and structured explanations**  
- Cover both **fundamentals and advanced concepts**  
- Serve as a **reference guide** for security professionals and learners  
- Promote **ethical hacking and defensive awareness**

---

## 🧠 Learning Philosophy

This project focuses on **understanding how attacks work**, not just how to execute them.  
Each topic aims to explain:

- **Why** a technique exists  
- **How** it works conceptually  
- **Where** it applies  
- **What** defenders should monitor or fix
